## Label semantics

The repository's PhiUSIIL provenance note (`docs/FINAL_ML_PASS.md`) identifies the source schema as `1 = legitimate`, `0 = phishing`. The preparation step explicitly maps that to VIGIL's `0 = legitimate`, `1 = phishing`; the mapping is recorded in `dataset_quality.json`. Every Phishing.Database row is assigned `1` and marked with source `phishing_database_active`.

## Overlap and role

After VIGIL canonicalization, 9,017 Phishing.Database rows overlap PhiUSIIL and are excluded. Of the remaining feed rows, 620,437 are added to training and 154,931 are retained as a deterministic external holdout. The holdout is phishing-only, so it cannot estimate false-positive rate.

## Final data

Training rows: 854,870. External validation rows: 154,931. Random and registered-domain-aware evaluation splits are performed by the existing training pipeline after this preparation step.
